WATER SURVEILLANCE PROTOCOL

Standard Operating Protocol • Quality Surveillance & Safety Standards

  • Microbiological & Chemical Surveillance: Ensure all water quality testing adheres to current national haemodialysis guidelines and AAMI/ISO standards.
  • Certified Laboratory Reports: Upload only official, certified laboratory result sheets in PDF format. Uploading Document 1 (Certified Result) is mandatory.
  • Immediate Corrective Action: Any bacterial count or endotoxin level exceeding acceptable clinical action thresholds must be reported immediately to the Nephrologist and BEMS engineering team.
